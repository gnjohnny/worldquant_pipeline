CANDIDATE 1 (54fb2629): Daytime Institutional Accumulation vs Overnight Noise Spread
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset: pv1 (100% matrix coverage across TOP3000)
Economic Rationale: Empirical microstructure research (Lou, Polk, and Skouras 2019) documents that informed institutional order flow executes during regular market hours, driving persistent daytime price trends ((close - open) / open). In contrast, overnight price gaps ((open - ts_delay(close, 1)) / ts_delay(close, 1)) are dominated by retail liquidity noise and sentiment overreaction that rapidly mean-reverts. Subtracting the overnight gap from the daytime return isolates genuine institutional accumulation with 100% instrument coverage across TOP3000.

Expression:
group_rank(ts_decay_linear((close - open) / open - (open - ts_delay(close, 1)) / ts_delay(close, 1), 10), subindustry) - 0.5
